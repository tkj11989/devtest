Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy applies to all apps and platforms from PraYukTech ("we", "us"), including ExamSuit, KnowledgeVerse and WordQuest, and any future apps we release.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: such as name and email if you create an account or contact us.
  • Usage data: app interactions, quiz or practice progress, and crash or performance diagnostics.
  • Device data: device model, OS version and a device identifier used for app functionality and analytics.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve our apps and platform
  • To save your progress and personalise your experience
  • To respond to support requests
  • To keep our services secure and prevent abuse

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers that help us run the apps (for example hosting, analytics, authentication or advertising providers), or when required by law.

4. Third-party services

Our apps may use third-party services such as Google Play Services, Firebase and Google AdMob. These providers have their own privacy policies.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them, with the exception of KnowledgeVerse and WordQuest, which are designed for a wide age range and have special child protections (see sections 10 and 11). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Email: Info@prayuktech.com

10. KnowledgeVerse (learning game)

This section describes how the KnowledgeVerse Android app (package com.prayuktech.knowledgeverse) handles information. Where it differs from the sections above, this section applies to KnowledgeVerse.

  • No account: KnowledgeVerse needs no account, email address or real name. Your nickname, emoji avatar, age band (for example 5–8, 9–12, 13–15, 16–18 or Adult; we never ask for a date of birth), progress, coins, achievements, shop items, language and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a server that receives this data.
  • Usage statistics: simple anonymous counters (such as the number of sessions or questions answered) are kept on your device only and can be turned off in Settings. Feedback or question reports you write also stay on your device unless you send them to us yourself.
  • Permissions: the app asks only for Internet and Network state, used to load ads. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No real-money purchases: the in-game Shop uses only coins earned by playing or by optionally watching an ad. We do not collect payment details.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): the app shows banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between missions and optional rewarded ads (for a hint, a second try or coins). Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Personalised ads are off by default, can be changed by teens and adults in Settings, and are always off for child profiles. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone’s Settings. Ads never appear during a question.
  • Children: for profiles under 13 the app marks ad requests as child-directed and under the age of consent, limits ad content to the “G” rating, turns social features off until a grown-up allows them, and lets a parent switch off optional ads in Parental controls. A simple parental gate protects ad and social features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through KnowledgeVerse.
  • Friend codes and leaderboards: friend codes are shared manually, and leaderboards are local to your device. No account or server is involved.
  • Your choices: in Settings you can download your data (Download my data) and erase everything on the device (Delete all my data). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings also removes it. Because we do not receive this data, we cannot recover it for you.

11. WordQuest (word puzzle game)

This section describes how the WordQuest Android app (package com.prayuktech.wordquest) handles information. Where it differs from the sections above, this section applies to WordQuest.

  • No account: WordQuest needs no account, email address, name or age. Your progress, stars, coins, achievements, stickers, themes and outfits you unlock, daily-challenge streak, chosen language and settings are stored only on your device. The current version does not send this data to us, and we do not run a server that receives it.
  • Usage and crash reports: Settings → Privacy has a switch to share anonymous usage and crash reports. It is off by default, and a parent or player can turn it on. When it is on, reports contain app events and counts (such as a level being completed) and crash information, and no name or email. They are handled by Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics).
  • Permissions: the app asks only for Internet and network state, used to load ads, and uses the Google advertising ID for ads (see below). It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Speech: the app can read a word aloud using your device’s built-in text-to-speech. It does not record any audio.
  • Purchases: coins are earned only by playing. There is an optional one-time “Remove ads” purchase handled entirely by Google Play, protected by a parent check. We do not see or store your payment details.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): WordQuest shows banner ads on menu screens, a native ad on the level-complete screen, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for free hints or to double the coins you earned). Ads never appear while you are solving a puzzle. Ad requests are marked as child-directed and as coming from users under the age of consent, ad content is limited to the “G” rating, and only non-personalised ads are requested. Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings, and removing ads in the app stops them.
  • Children: WordQuest is a learning game that children may play. We do not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.
  • Your choices: you can turn usage and crash reports on or off at any time in Settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes everything stored on your device. Because we do not receive this data, we cannot recover it for you. For any question, email Info@prayuktech.com.
