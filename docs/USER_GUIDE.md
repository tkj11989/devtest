# KnowledgeVerse — User Guide

## Getting started
1. **Onboarding** – choose a nickname (never your real name), age group, language, interests, difficulty and hero. Or tap *Skip — play as guest*.
2. **Home** – shows your level, XP, coins and streak. Tap **Continue playing** to jump into your next mission; **Resume** appears if you left mid-mission.
3. **Worlds** – the map has eight islands. Locked islands show the player level needed. Earn XP to unlock them — no purchase or ad is ever required.

## Playing a mission
* Each world has 10 missions (Beginner → Expert) and a **Boss**. Missions unlock in order; the boss unlocks after all 10.
* Question types: multiple choice, typing answers, tap-to-order (sentences, letters, timelines), matching, drag-and-drop sorting, lab experiments, budget selection, memory, word hunt, map pins, combination locks.
* **Hint** (5 🪙, or free via an optional ad) · **Report** a wrong question · **Exit** any time — progress is saved.
* Stars: 60% = ★, 80% = ★★, 95% = ★★★ (boss: 70 / 82 / 95%). Replay to improve stars; only improvements pay extra rewards.
* Timed missions show a countdown; unanswered questions count as incorrect.

## Rewards
XP → player levels → new worlds. Coins buy hints. Achievements, daily login rewards (7-day cycle), streak milestones (3/7/14/30 days) and world-completion bonuses add more.

## Daily challenges
Four challenges every day (Math, Language, Reasoning, rotating World Spotlight), 3 questions each. They don’t change when you reopen the app. Finish all four for a bonus. Play any activity daily to grow your streak.

## Knowledge Challenge
Quick (5), Standard (10), Expert (15, timed) questions mixed across your unlocked worlds, with a subject-by-subject score.

## Friends & leaderboards (optional)
Personal bests, subject ranking and a weekly challenge. **Friend challenges** use a short code (e.g. `KV-S-1A2B3C`) — friends get the same questions. No chat, no messaging, no personal information.

## Settings
Music / sound effects (separate), reduced motion, text size, difficulty, currency, privacy, download or delete all your data.

## For parents
*Me → Parental controls* (PIN or grown-up question): friend challenges on/off, optional rewarded ads on/off, daily time limit, settings lock. Under-13 profiles have social features off by default and get child-directed, non-personalised ads.
