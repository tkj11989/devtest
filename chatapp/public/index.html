                  Encryption

                  Messages you type in ChatApp are end-to-end encrypted: only you and the people in the chat can read them, not even the server. Each device below holds its own key and can read messages sent after it was set up. Remove a device you no longer use or recognise.

                    Messages sent by apps through the API, and automatic business replies, are not end-to-end encrypted and are marked with an open padlock. Calls are encrypted directly between the devices. Status updates are not end-to-end encrypted.
