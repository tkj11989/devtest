KnowledgeVerse

Android

A learning adventure for curious minds of every age. Travel through 11 worlds, from Math, Science, History, Space and Geography to Sports, Money, Mind Lab and Tech & Coding, with 1,000 levels in each. Enjoy daily challenges, rewards and a coin shop. Available in English and Hindi, and it works offline with no account needed.

WordQuest

Android

A word puzzle adventure that teaches English. Unscramble the letters to find each word, then build a correct sentence with grammar blocks. Play 5,000 levels across 50 worlds and learn over 3,000 words, with daily challenges, hints, and coins to unlock themes, map designs and outfits for your mascot. The menus follow your phone’s language, with 14 languages including Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Spanish, French, German and Russian. It works offline and needs no account.
